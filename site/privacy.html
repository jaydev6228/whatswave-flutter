WhatsWave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

WhatsWave is a messaging app. This policy explains exactly what we collect, what we cannot see, and what you can ask us to delete. It describes what the app actually does, not what we would like you to assume.

The short version. Your message text, your attachments and the files you send are end-to-end encrypted — we cannot read them, and neither can anyone who obtains our servers' contents. We can see who you message, when, and how often. Your phone's address book is never uploaded.

3. Your address book is not uploaded

This differs from how most messaging apps work, so it is worth stating plainly.

When you grant contacts permission, WhatsWave reads your address book on your device to find which of your contacts already use the app. To do that, it checks numbers against our directory one at a time, using a shortened match key derived from the number rather than the number itself.

We never upload, store or retain a copy of your contact list. Your contacts' names never leave your phone. We do store your own number in that directory so your friends can find you.

You can decline contacts permission entirely and still use the app; you will simply have to start chats by entering a number.

4. End-to-end encryption

Message text and attachments are encrypted on your device using the Signal Protocol before they ever reach our servers, and are decrypted only on the recipient's device. Attachment files are separately encrypted with a key that travels inside the encrypted message.

The practical consequence: we cannot read your messages, cannot hand them to anyone who asks, and cannot recover them for you if you lose your device. This applies to one-to-one chats and group chats.

You can verify a conversation is genuinely encrypted by comparing the safety number shown in the contact's info screen with the one on their device. If a contact's safety number changes, the app tells you.

5. What encryption does not cover

We would rather state this than let you assume otherwise. The following are visible to us because they are needed to deliver messages at all:

  • Who you message, and who is in your groups
  • When each message was sent, delivered and read
  • How often and how much you message
  • Group names, group descriptions and profile information
  • Your phone number and when you last connected

Additionally, voice and video calls are not end-to-end encrypted. Calls are carried by LiveKit, our calling provider, which handles the audio and video in transit. Calls are encrypted on the network but LiveKit's infrastructure can technically access the media stream. We do not record calls.

Stories/status posts and community posts are also not end-to-end encrypted at this time.

7. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure and may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required by law, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep things

  • Messages remain until deleted by you or another participant. Deleting for everyone removes the content from our servers.
  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics are retained for up to 14 months.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • Access a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

Email jaydevbaloliya@gmail.com from the address or phone number associated with your account. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR applies and you may complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies. If you are in California, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA — note that we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there.

Deleting your account

Email us from your registered address or number and we will delete your account, profile, encryption keys and server-side data within 30 days. Messages you already sent to other people remain on their devices — we cannot reach into someone else's phone.

10. Children

WhatsWave is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it. Some jurisdictions set a higher minimum age; where that is the case, the higher age applies.

11. Security

Beyond end-to-end encryption, we use transport encryption for everything in transit, encrypt locally cached messages on your device, and store encryption keys in your device's secure hardware keystore. The app offers optional biometric lock; your biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to jaydevbaloliya@gmail.com.

12. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
